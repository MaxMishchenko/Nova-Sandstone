Design Ideas

Nova Sandstone's light grey tones pair beautifully with a variety of materials to elevate any interior design:

  • Modern Minimalist Homes:

    Complement with white or beige walls, glass furniture, and brushed nickel light fixtures for a clean, serene space.

  • Scandinavian-Inspired Interiors:

    Pair with light wood furniture, linen curtains, and natural fiber rugs for a cozy Nordic feel.

  • Urban Lofts:

    Balance exposed brick or concrete with matte black metal accents and plush neutral textiles for warmth.

  • Coastal Retreats:

    Use soft blue or sage green decor, rattan furniture, and light, sheer curtains to create a breezy, relaxed vibe.

  • Contemporary Spaces:

    Highlight with quartz or marble countertops, metallic accents in brass or chrome, and modern pendant lighting.
